Welcome to Service Optimiser

What this tool does

Service Optimiser helps you calculate the best production mix based on your inventory, product definitions, and sustainability goals. You can:

  • 📦 Maximise profit without exceeding inventory
  • 🌱 Minimise carbon footprint
  • ⚖️ Balance multiple objectives

How to use it

  1. Set up your inventory and stock levels
  2. Define products with cost, profit, and CO₂e
  3. Choose optimisation goals and constraints
  4. View and export the optimal production mix

Start Here

How to use this tool:
  1. Inventory Setup: Define your available resources (e.g., materials, manhours) and their stock levels. Save or load inventory as XML.
  2. Product Setup: Define each product's cost, profit, unit, and carbon footprint. Save or load products as XML.
  3. Optimisation Parameters: Choose your objective (e.g., maximise profit or minimise carbon footprint) and set minimum production constraints.
  4. Results: View the optimal production mix calculated using linear programming. Export or reuse results as needed.

You can navigate between screens at any time. Your data is stored locally in your browser during the session.
